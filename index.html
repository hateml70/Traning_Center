Enrollment Path

From application to job offer.

The same four stages for every trade, timed around work and family schedules.

01

Apply

Submit a short application and sit a 20-minute placement conversation — no exam, no fee.

02

Foundations

Two weeks of safety, math, and tool fundamentals shared across all trades.

03

Hands-On Labs

The bulk of the program: supervised work on real equipment in small crews of six.

04

Placement

Our employer network interviews graduating cohorts before the final week ends.

Why Ridgeline

Built like a shop, not a school.

Taught by journeymen

Every instructor works or worked the trade full-time — no career academics.

Evenings & weekends

Every program runs around a day job, with cohorts starting quarterly.

Employer network

140+ regional contractors and plants hire directly from our graduating cohorts.

Financing that fits

Payment plans, employer sponsorship, and workforce-grant paperwork handled on-site.
